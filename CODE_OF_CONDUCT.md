# Code of Conduct

## Belqen Community

Welcome to the Belqen community.

I want Belqen Discussions, Issues, pull requests, and other community spaces to remain respectful, constructive, and useful for everyone interested in the project.

By participating in the Belqen community, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.

## Expected Behavior

When participating in the community, please:

* Treat other community members with respect.
* Keep discussions constructive and relevant.
* Be open to different opinions, approaches, and levels of experience.
* Provide useful and respectful feedback.
* Explain technical disagreements without attacking the person behind them.
* Give appropriate credit to other people's work.
* Respect the project's licensing requirements.
* Follow the **Belqen Custom Non-Commercial License (BCNCL)** when using, modifying, distributing, or creating derivative works based on Belqen.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
* Discrimination or hateful conduct toward other community members.
* Threats or deliberate attempts to harm, disrupt, or abuse other community members.
* Publishing private or personal information without permission.
* Deliberately misleading or impersonating other community members.
* Spam, excessive self-promotion, or unrelated advertising.
* Malicious behavior intended to disrupt the project or its community.
* Deliberate attempts to bypass repository, platform, or license restrictions.
* Using or distributing Belqen source code in ways that violate the BCNCL.

## Technical Disagreements

Technical disagreements are expected in an open development project.

Different approaches, implementations, and opinions are welcome. However, disagreements should remain focused on the technical subject rather than becoming personal.

A contribution or idea can be criticized without attacking the person who proposed it.

## License Compliance

The **Belqen Custom Non-Commercial License (BCNCL)** applies to the Belqen project and its qualifying derivative works.

Participation in the Belqen community does not grant additional rights to use, modify, distribute, or process Belqen source code.

Community members must comply with the BCNCL, including its restrictions regarding commercial use, attribution, derivative works, and artificial intelligence processing.

Please read the [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) file for the complete license terms.

## Enforcement

I may remove, edit, lock, or otherwise moderate content that violates this Code of Conduct or the applicable repository rules.

Depending on the circumstances, repeated or serious violations may result in restrictions on participation in the Belqen community.

Enforcement decisions may take into account the severity and context of the behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately.

For security vulnerabilities, please follow the reporting process described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) rather than publicly posting sensitive vulnerability details.

For other community conduct issues, you may contact me through the contact information provided in the repository.

Please do not publicly expose private information or sensitive details when reporting an issue.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in Belqen community spaces, including:

* GitHub Discussions
* Issues
* Pull requests
* Repository-related conversations
* Other community spaces officially associated with Belqen

## Acknowledgement

By participating in the Belqen community, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and respect the rights and restrictions established by the BCNCL.

Thank you for helping keep the Belqen community respectful, constructive, and focused on development.
